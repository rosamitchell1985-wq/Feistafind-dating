Your safety matters

Take Your Time. Connect Safely.

Good relationships are never in a hurry. A few simple habits keep you in control.

  • Keep conversations on-platform initially
  • Never send money to someone you met online
  • Never share passwords or financial information
  • Be cautious about sharing your home address
  • Report suspicious behavior
  • Block users when necessary
  • Meet in public places
  • Tell someone you trust about your plans
