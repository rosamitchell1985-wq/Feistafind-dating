Access for everyone

Accessibility Statement

A community only works if everyone can take part. Accessibility is a feature, not an afterthought.

Last updated: 2026

Our approach

Feistafind is designed for adults 50+, which means designing for the full range of vision, hearing, motor, and cognitive differences that come with being human. This prototype follows recognised good practices, and a production service should be audited against WCAG 2.2 Level AA.

What this site does

  • Keyboard navigation — every interactive element is reachable and usable by keyboard, with a visible focus indicator
  • Semantic HTML — proper header, navigation, main, section, and footer landmarks, with a logical heading hierarchy
  • Alt text — meaningful descriptions on photos and images
  • Color contrast — text and controls are designed to meet WCAG AA contrast ratios, and colour is never the only way information is conveyed
  • Readable typography — a large base font size and generous line spacing as the default
  • Form labels — every input has a visible label and clear error messages
  • Reduced motion — the site honours your system's reduced-motion preference
  • Responsive design — usable at small screen sizes and high zoom without horizontal scrolling

Honest limitations

This statement describes intent and current practice — it is not a certification, and we do not claim one. Accessibility is ongoing work, and some areas may still fall short.
