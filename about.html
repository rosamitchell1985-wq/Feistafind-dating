About Feistafind

Meaningful Connections. New Beginnings.

Later life can be one of the richest times for friendship and love — but meeting people gets harder when the usual places fall away. Feistafind exists to make it easier, warmer, and safer.

Our purpose

Feistafind is a community platform where adults 50 and over can meet for friendship, companionship, dating, and meaningful relationships. We believe connection at this stage of life deserves a space built for it — unhurried, respectful, and free of games.

Our mission is simple: help people find good company, at their own pace, with their safety and dignity at the center of every design decision.

What we believe

  • Respect. Every member is a whole person, not a profile to be swiped past. Courtesy is the baseline, and our community guidelines enforce it.
  • Privacy. You decide what to share and with whom. We collect only what we need, and we never ask for information we can't justify. See our privacy policy.
  • Safety. Blocking, reporting, and clear guidance are built in — not bolted on. Our Safety Center is always one click away.
  • Accessibility. Large readable text, high contrast, keyboard-friendly navigation, and simple layouts — because a community only works if everyone can use it. Read our accessibility statement.
  • Honesty. We don't promise matches, we don't invent testimonials, and we don't pressure anyone. Trust is earned with clarity, not claims.
